NEW TO THE GREAT BOOKS?

Start here

Three paths through this library, by how much time you have. Every stop links the book to buy, the Ascend discussions to follow, and the free guides. Rule of thumb from Ascend itself: read the text first, then the guide — the goal is to make you a reader, not a guide-dependent.

1 · The taste (a weekend to 2 weeks)

First evening: Bartleby, the Scrivener. Thirty pages, one unsettling clerk who "would prefer not to" — Ascend's key to the modern age. Story page → · Buy →
Second evening: Plato's First Alcibiades. The dialogue antiquity handed beginners first — young Alcibiades invited to the philosophic life. Video + 20-question guide. Plato page → · Video →
If you're hooked: read which dialogue to try next, or jump to track 2.

2 · One epic (8–12 weeks)

Pick your epic: Odyssey or Inferno. The Odyssey (Fagles) with the 12-week re-read path — translation guide, Calypso, Penelope, Telemachus. Odyssey page → · Buy Fagles →
Or Dante for a season: Inferno (Hollander) in 7 episodes, then Purgatorio the next Lent. Inferno page → · Buy Hollander →
Want company? Online Great Books seminars the same books monthly; Close Reads covered the Odyssey back in 2019.

3 · The full ascent (1–2 years, in Ascend's order)

Semester 1 — Homer: Iliad (24 weeks) → Odyssey (24 weeks). The foundation everything else quotes.
Semester 2 — Tragedy & Dante: Oresteia → Theban plays + Bacchae → Inferno. Justice, law, and Hell.
Semester 3 — Plato: Socratic foundations → Republic — live now, join the tracker. Read week by week with 20+ scholars starting Oct 6.
Capstone: Purgatorio → Paradiso (Lent 2027). End where Dante ends: the love that moves the sun.

Detours worth taking

Love Lewis? After Ascend's Narnia essays, do Pints With Jack Season 3 (Till We Have Faces) alongside Word on Fire's read of the same book.
Love Rome? After the Greek plays, Cost of Glory's Plutarch Lives are the Roman sequel — start with Cato or Caesar.
Love novels? Close Reads' year-by-year list is a decade-long syllabus: Jayber Crow → Gilead → Kristin Lavransdatter → Beloved.
