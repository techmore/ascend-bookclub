The Bacchae

Euripides · 405 BC · a play on mania, virtue, and the divine

Dionysus returns to Thebes and King Pentheus answers ecstasy with prison cells — until the god leads him, dressed as a maenad, to the mountain where his own mother waits. Madness, piety, gore, and civilizational collapse: Euripides' final warning about a city that refuses reverence.
