Word on Fire Book Club (Club 451)

Bishop Barron's Word on Fire Institute · wordonfire.org

The Institute's reading community ("Club 451" to its founders) works through spiritual classics — Benedict's Rule, Julian of Norwich, Pope Benedict XVI — alongside novels that train the moral imagination: Walker Percy, C.S. Lewis, Flannery O'Connor. Discussion threads, Zoom meetings, and webinars included with membership, plus the Word on Fire Classics hardcover line (Augustine's Confessions, Chesterton's Orthodoxy, Newman's Development, Merton).
