Word on Fire Book Club

Bishop Barron's Word on Fire Institute · wordonfire.org

The Institute's reading community ("Club 451" to its founders) works through spiritual classics and novels that train the moral imagination — discussion threads while you read, a live Zoom when you finish, plus webinars with authors and scholars. The Word on Fire Classics hardcovers (Merton, Newman, Chesterton, Augustine's Confessions in the Sheed translation) make a handsome shelf.

Seasonal reads + discussion threads + Zoom/webinars (Institute membership)
