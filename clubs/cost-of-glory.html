Cost of Glory

Alex Petkas · costofglory.com

A former professor of ancient philosophy retells Plutarch's Parallel Lives — "the most influential biographies ever written" — dramatically for modern listeners, with Xenophon, Plato, and Caesar alongside. Statesmen, generals, and founders as moral education: the Roman complement to Ascend's Greek arc. Extras: the Rostra speaking community, the Classical Society, leadership retreats, and a Substack with maps and analysis.
