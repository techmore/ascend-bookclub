Pints With Jack

David Bates, Matt Bush & Andrew Lazo · pintswithjack.com

Literally born from a book club: the hosts started reading C.S. Lewis together over a pint and kept going — nine seasons and counting. Each season takes one Lewis book chapter by chapter every Tuesday, with "After Hours" scholar interviews, Half Pint videos, a Patreon Slack community, and a worldwide directory of Lewis groups. The natural next step after Ascend's Narnia and Lewis essays.

Format
Weekly chapter discussions + After Hours interviews + newsletter + livestreams
Community
Patreon Slack, C.S. Lewis Reading Day, groups directory, shop & merch
