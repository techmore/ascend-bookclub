Close Reads

Goldberry Studios · David Kern, Heidi White, Sean Johnson · closereads.substack.com

A book-club podcast "for the incurable reader," born at the CiRCE Institute and now produced by Goldberry Studios. Amateurs in the best sense — hospitable, nerdy, contemplative conversations through whole books, a few chapters at a time. Twelve books a year, chosen publicly in "The Great Winnowing"; recent series include Huck Finn and Moby Dick.

Format
Weekly chapter discussions + Substack community + live road events
